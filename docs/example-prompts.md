# Example prompts

Original starting points, not pre-rendered films or quality guarantees. Begin with a 30-second draft and match the UI settings to your request.

## Sharing shade

> Create a 30-second stylized 3D story about two neighbors sharing shade on a warm day. Use two original adult characters with consistent clothing and voices, a sunny courtyard, three clear camera shots, gentle English narration, and readable captions. End with “A little kindness goes a long way.” Use ambient sound and no music.

## The patient gardener

> Make a 60-second storybook animation about an adult gardener who waters a seed each day and learns patience. Keep the same apron and hat throughout, use one garden, and change the daylight to show time passing. Use warm English narration, outdoor sounds, captions, and no music. End when the first leaf appears.

## A curious robot

> Create a 30-second playful Blender animation in landscape format. One small original robot notices a rolling ball, tries twice to catch it, and finally stops it with a cardboard ramp. Keep the same robot model and materials in every shot. Use three camera angles, gentle mechanical effects, and no dialogue or music.

## An Islamic moral story

> Create a 60-second anime-inspired 3D fictional story about an adult shopkeeper returning a lost purse. Show the choice, the return, and the owner's relief. Keep the two original adult characters consistent. Use calm English narration, captions, street ambience, and no music. Communicate honesty through the action. Do not invent or quote scripture.

## A longer chaptered story

> Plan a 10-minute fictional adventure about two original adult travelers restoring a village water wheel. Begin with a complete script, character and voice bible, and scene manifest. Reuse one shared Blender cast library. Save short scene checkpoints and create a representative preview before full rendering. Use English dialogue, narration, captions, and original ambience. Estimate render time and report limitations honestly.

Longer productions can require substantial local rendering time and several Codex sessions. Reusable assets support continuity; review each chapter before assembly.
