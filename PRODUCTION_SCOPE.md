# Product scope

Prompt Video Studio is a local, single-user Windows application that asks Codex to create editable stories and videos with free local tools.

The workflow supports official Codex sign-in, duration targets from 30 seconds to 20 minutes, Blender animation, FFmpeg assembly, optional narration, sound, captions, progress, approvals, and saved-thread continuation.

Productions should save scripts, character and voice bibles, reusable cast assets, scene checkpoints, final MP4, editable sources, captions when requested, thumbnail, credits, and verification. See [PRODUCTION.md](PRODUCTION.md).

Keep the extracted app directory outside cloud-synced folders when local storage is desired. Reuse installed tools before downloading free dependencies from official sources through normal tools and approvals.

This alpha cannot promise professional animation, perfect continuity, a specific render time, or completion of every long film. Codex access and account limits are separate. Computer-use capabilities depend on the installed environment.

The product does not include a paid video provider, separate video API-key interface, hosted multi-user service, unattended scheduler, or automatic social publishing.
